Privacy Policy

This policy covers the personal assistant notification service operated by David Dent (the registered Brand).

What we collect

The mobile phone number of the account owner, the owner's SMS opt-in status, and the content of messages exchanged with the service's number. No other personal information is collected, and no one other than the account owner receives messages.

How it is used

The phone number and opt-in status are used only to deliver notifications the owner asked for and to honor STOP and HELP requests. Message contents are used only to act on the owner's replies. Data is stored on the owner's own computer and with the carrier service provider (Twilio) as required to deliver messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with the carrier service provider as necessary to send and receive messages.

Your choices

Reply STOP to end messages at any time; reply HELP for help. Questions: contact David Dent at the email on file with the carrier account.
