SMS notifications — consent

This phone number sends text messages from a personal assistant system to one recipient only: the account owner, who operates the system and this number. No other person receives messages from it, and no numbers are collected or marketed to.

How consent is given: the account owner opts in by texting the number first, or by enabling notifications in the assistant's settings. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

To stop: reply STOP to end messages. Reply HELP for help. No information is shared with third parties.

Terms and conditions are on the separate terms page. Contact: the account owner, at the email on file with the carrier account.
